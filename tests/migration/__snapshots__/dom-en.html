FAQ

What to know before starting

Do you only work with manufacturing and technical companies?

No. Technical companies are one area where I have deep experience. The same automation principles also apply to services, hospitality, project management, nonprofits, content businesses and internal administration.

Do we have to replace our current software?

Often not. We first look for ways to connect email, calendars, CRM, documents, accounting or operational systems and add an automation and AI layer on top.

Do we need to start with a large project?

No. I prefer one concrete process, a small pilot and a measurable result. We expand only what proves useful.

Can AI act completely on its own?

For routine steps, yes. For sensitive decisions I design permissions, checkpoints and human approval.

What will it cost?

Pricing depends on the process, integrations and pilot scope. You receive a proposal before work starts. Sending an inquiry does not place a paid order.

Will we pay for additional tools?

Sometimes, for example for software, hosting or AI model calls. The proposal distinguishes implementation fees from ongoing costs. We agree on services and budget in advance.

What about our data and access?

Before the pilot we agree on the data, who can access it and which services process it. Do not include passwords or confidential customer documents in the initial contact form.

What is handed over and who handles failures?

Documentation, access, rights to deliverables and ongoing support are agreed in the proposal. The design also covers failure handling and steps a person can take over. Round-the-clock support is not included automatically.
